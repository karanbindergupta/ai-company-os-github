`growth-lead` and `product-lead` review: what the metrics actually show against what was predicted,
which assumptions held and which failed, what users do that nobody expected, and what the evidence
says to build next.

Write findings to `.ai-company/knowledge/lessons.md` and feed the roadmap.

**Report what the data shows, including when it contradicts the plan.** A post-launch review that
confirms everything went well is usually not a review.
