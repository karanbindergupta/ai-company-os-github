Focus: **$ARGUMENTS**

```bash
python3 scripts/company.py phase-start discovery
```

Dispatch `product-lead` as `product-discovery-specialist`, and `researcher` as
`customer-researcher` — **in parallel**, they are independent.

Discovery must establish, with evidence:
- what problem is real, and how severely it is felt
- who actually has it (a segment, not a demographic)
- what they do about it today
- why existing solutions fail them
- **the riskiest assumption in the founder's idea**
- whether the proposed solution is differentiated at all

Artifacts: `.ai-company/product/discovery.md`, `.ai-company/product/assumptions.md`

If discovery invalidates the founder's premise, **say so**. Escalate to the CEO. That is a
successful discovery, not a failed one.
