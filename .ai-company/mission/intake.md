# Founder Intake

## Summary
Founder supplied an industry and a one-line idea. No constraints beyond solo founder, pre-revenue.
This is a **dry-run mission** used to exercise the organization, not a real venture.

## As given
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Industry | Local food marketplace |
| Idea | A marketplace connecting customers with independent local home chefs |
| Constraints | Solo founder, pre-revenue |

## What the founder did NOT specify
These are the company's to determine, not the founder's to be asked:
geography, customer segment, which side of the marketplace is hardest to acquire, delivery model,
regulatory posture, pricing, take rate, or platform surface.

## Immediate observations for the CEO
1. **Two-sided marketplace** — the cold-start problem is the central risk, not the software.
2. **Food safety regulation is likely the binding constraint**, not product features. In the US
   this is cottage food law, which varies by state.
3. The idea names a solution, not a problem. Discovery must establish the problem before scope.
