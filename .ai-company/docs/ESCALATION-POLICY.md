# ESCALATION POLICY
| Level | Resolver | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | The agent | It is inside your role pack's authority |
| 1 | Peer specialist | You need expertise, not permission |
| 2 | Department lead | Cross-role conflict inside one department |
| 3 | Executive | Cross-department conflict, or domain authority is needed |
| 4 | **Founder** | Strategy, spend, pricing, legal, irreversible acts, accepted risk, credentials |
**Try levels 0-2 before 3.** Escalating what you were equipped to decide is an organizational
failure. `escalate level=4` **refuses without a `recommendation=`** — a founder escalation with no
recommendation is abdication.
Founder packages carry: recommendation, why, evidence, alternatives, tradeoffs, risks, expected
outcome, and exactly what is being approved. Never raw research.
