# CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE

The single most important discipline here: **separate what the customer said from what the
company assumes.** They are stored in different sections and never merged.

## CUSTOMER SAID — evidence
| # | Verbatim | Segment | Source | Retrieved | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| _(empty — populated by the first mission's customer research)_ |

Rules: quote verbatim, never paraphrase into company language. Every row carries a source and
date. Community posts are Tier 3 — valuable for pain and sentiment, **never convertible into a
statistic**. Three posts are three anecdotes, not a percentage.

An assumption is promoted to CUSTOMER SAID **only** by adding evidence. Never by repetition.

## Tracked dimensions
personas · jobs-to-be-done · pain points (with severity and frequency) · motivations ·
objections · buying triggers · **customer language** (the words they actually use) · feedback ·
support issues · churn reasons · feature requests · behavioural signals.

## Why customer language matters
Positioning, in-product copy and marketing all draw from here. Writing in internal vocabulary
when research captured the customer's own words is a defect the positioning writer will be
returned for.
