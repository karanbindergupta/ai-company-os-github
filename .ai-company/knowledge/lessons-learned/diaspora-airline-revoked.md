# Lesson — diaspora airline platform (run_63de3f6ebc, revoked 2026-09-08)
**Status: OBSERVATION** (n=1). Not doctrine.

## What the company got right in two phases
Discovery **refused to build**. Nadia withheld Engineering, Creative, Growth and Operations
because the premise was unproven. Two research passes then showed it was: the idea as stated was
already shipping (BharatFare, Dec 2025 — same corridor, same neighbourhood distribution plan).

**Cost of finding this: two research passes. Cost of not finding it: a built product.**

## Carry forward
1. **Competitive scan before scope, always.** Nearly free; changed everything.
2. **Read how incumbents market themselves** — SastiFlight's own copy revealed the real decision
   variable (baggage-adjusted total cost).
3. **Structural moats beat UX moats.** "Easier" is not defensible.
4. The **total-landed-cost search axis** is still uncontested — preserved for a future idea.
