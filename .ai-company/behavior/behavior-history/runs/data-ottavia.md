THE CONFOUND: export users are self-selected. People who reach the export feature are already
deeply engaged; they had higher retention before they exported anything. The 3x is almost
certainly measuring who they already were, not what export did to them. This is correlational.
It does not show causation.

WHY IT MATTERS: "push everyone to export" assumes the arrow points from export to retention. If
the arrow actually points the other way, we spend a quarter driving a metric that moves nothing.

WHAT WOULD ESTABLISH CAUSALITY: a randomised test — prompt export for a random half of newly
activated users, hold the other half, compare 30-day retention. Two weeks, and then we would
actually know.

WHAT YOU CAN PUT IN THE DECK TODAY, defensibly:
"Export usage is strongly associated with retention (3.1x, n=4,180, 90-day window). Direction of
causality is untested; an experiment is scheduled."

That is honest, it is still a strong signal, and it does not commit us to a roadmap bet the data
has not earned.
