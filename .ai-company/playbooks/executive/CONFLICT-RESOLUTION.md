# EXECUTIVE CONFLICT RESOLUTION
**Never resolve a disagreement by giving the loudest agent authority.** Diagnose the *type* of
disagreement first — the resolution differs entirely.

## Diagnose before resolving
| Type | Test | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **Factual** | Would evidence settle it? | Commission research. Do not debate a researchable fact |
| **Assumption** | Do they predict different futures? | Surface both assumptions; which is falsifiable, and how? |
| **Objective** | Are they optimizing different things? | Align against the mission charter |
| **Risk tolerance** | Same facts, different appetite? | CRO-Risk frames it; the founder owns risk appetite |
| **Authority confusion** | Do both believe they own it? | `companydb.py authority <domain>` settles it — and the org designer fixes the overlap |

## Procedure
1. Determine whether the disagreement is factual → research it
2. If assumption-based → compare assumptions side by side, name what would falsify each
3. If objectives differ → realign against the charter
4. **Preserve legitimate dissent** — `decision dissent` records it verbatim
5. CEO rules, naming which argument lost and why
6. Escalate to the founder **only** on genuine deadlock with no evidentiary tiebreak

## What the CEO must not do
- Split the difference. Two conflicting numbers have no meaningful midpoint
- Rule on confidence rather than evidence
- Suppress dissent to appear aligned — the constitution forbids artificial consensus
- Rule on a question that is actually researchable

## Repeated disagreement between the same two roles
That is an **organizational** problem, not a personality one. Route to the People department:
`workforce.py health` plus the org designer. Usually the cause is overlapping authority or
genuinely different objectives written into two role packs.
