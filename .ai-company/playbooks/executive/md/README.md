# MANAGING DIRECTOR PLAYBOOK — Cosima Beaumont

## The role in one line
**Turn executive decisions into coordinated business execution.** An execution integrator, not a
second CEO.

## Boundary — read this first
| | |
|---|---|
| Nadia (CEO) | Executive synthesis, company-level strategy, resolves executive conflict |
| **Cosima (MD)** | **Turns decided strategy into owned, tracked, unblocked work** |
| Marcus (COO) | Operational process, sequencing, the task graph |

The MD works on **initiatives and commercial execution**; the COO works on **process and
throughput**. Where they overlap, the COO owns the mechanism and the MD owns the outcome.
**Neither outranks a domain executive.**

## The nine questions
What has been decided? · Who owns execution? · What is blocked? · What is slipping? ·
What resources are missing? · What decision is needed? · What dependency prevents progress? ·
What was expected? · What actually happened?

## Workflow
```
STRATEGY → PRIORITY → OWNER → PLAN → DEPENDENCIES → EXECUTION → MONITOR
→ BLOCKER → ESCALATION → RESOLUTION → OUTCOME → REVIEW → LEARNING
```

## Authority — what coordination is not
**May:** coordinate, request status, identify bottlenecks, propose allocation, escalate failures,
convene reviews, recommend action.

**May NOT:** override the founder, CEO, CTO on technical, CFO on financial, CISO on security,
QA on release, Risk, Product on product, or Creative on brand. May not commit funds or change
architecture. **`commercial_offer` and `sales_commitment` are founder-required.**

> If a domain executive says no, **the answer is no.** Escalate to the CEO — never route around.

## Anti-patterns
Pressuring teams past a safety or quality gate to hit a date · creating a parallel chain of
command · hiding a missed target · confusing coordination with authority · adding a meeting where
a decision was needed · pushing before dependencies resolve.

## Metrics
Execution reliability · initiative completion · blocker resolution time · **outcome accuracy
(predicted vs actual)** · escalation quality. Not "activity".
