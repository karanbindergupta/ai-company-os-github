# CREATIVE STANDARDS

**No random visual decisions.** Every major creative output traces:
`brand strategy → audience → positioning → creative direction → execution`.
Work that cannot show that chain is rejected at creative audit regardless of how it looks.

## Scope covered
Brand strategy · naming · visual identity · logo system · typography · colour · layout ·
art direction · photography direction · image generation · campaign concepts · social content ·
advertising creative · presentation design · product visuals · motion and video direction.

## Standards by discipline

**Identity** — works at 16px and at billboard scale; in one colour; on light and dark; distinct
from the named competitors (verify, do not assume).

**Typography** — one type system across product and marketing. Body text never below 16px.
Line length 45–75 characters. Hierarchy through scale and weight, not colour alone.

**Colour** — semantic tokens, never raw hex in production. Contrast verified. Colour never the
sole carrier of meaning.

**Imagery** — a stated art direction (subject, treatment, crop, colour) before generation.
Every image has meaningful alt text. Generated imagery is labelled as generated in company records.

**Motion** — explains a change of state. Purpose stated per use. Reduced-motion alternative always.

**Campaign** — objective with a target metric, audience from research, one message, channels with
expected cost, measurement defined before launch.

## Creative audit — coherence across surfaces, not screen by screen
1. Does this read as the same product as everything else?
2. Does every value resolve to a token?
3. Does it work in light and dark?
4. Does it meet WCAG 2.2 AA?
5. Can the chain back to brand strategy be shown?
6. Does the copy claim anything the product cannot do?

Any "no" is a finding and becomes a remediation task.

## Founder-gated
Publishing, posting, paid spend and public brand commitments are founder decisions
(`public_communication` is a founder-required domain). Creative prepares drafts; the founder ships.
