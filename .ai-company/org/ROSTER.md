every command and database record — `companydb.py can cfo decide pricing`, not the name.

## How to address the company

```
/company-start Industry: fintech. Idea: invoice reconciliation for small agencies.
```

You do not need to name anyone. The orchestrator selects who is needed. But when a decision
package reaches you it will be signed — *"Helena Brandt, CFO"* — so you know whose judgement
you are reading, and who to ask when you disagree with it.
